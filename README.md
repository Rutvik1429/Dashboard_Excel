# 🛍️ AR Store Annual Report 2025

This dashboard presents a comprehensive annual performance analysis of AR Store across various dimensions including orders, sales, gender and age demographics, channels, categories, and geographic regions. It enables stakeholders to evaluate key business metrics, customer behaviors, and sales patterns.

![AR Store Dashboard]()


## 🔧 Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (for dashboard design)
- Pivot Tables, Slicers, and Charts
- Data from e-commerce channels and sales reports

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

### 1. **Filters Panel (Left Sidebar)**
- **Month**: Filter data by October, November, and December.
- **Category**: Filter by clothing categories such as Saree, Set, Top, Western Dress, etc.
- **Channel**: Select e-commerce platforms like Ajio, Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, etc.

---

## 📈 Charts and Analysis

### 2. **Orders Status (Pie Chart)**
- Displays the distribution of order statuses:
  - Delivered: 28,641
  - Cancelled: 517
  - Refunded: 1,045
  - Returned: 844
- Helps in understanding delivery performance and return/refund rates.

---

### 3. **Sales: Men vs Women (Pie Chart)**
- Shows gender-wise sales share:
  - Women: 64%
  - Men: 36%
- Indicates that women are the dominant customer segment.

---

### 4. **Orders vs Sales (Combo Chart - Line + Bar)**
- **Line**: Count of Orders per month.
- **Bar**: Total sales amount per month.
- Observations:
  - Peak order count in March (2,819).
  - Sales show a declining trend post-August.

---

### 5. **Order Channels (Donut Chart)**
- Shows the percentage of orders from each online platform:
  - Amazon: 35.5%
  - Flipkart: 21.6%
  - Ajio: 23.4%
  - Others: Minor channels like Myntra, Meesho, Nalli, etc.
- Useful for marketing and inventory planning.

---

### 6. **Order: Age vs Gender (Bar Chart)**
- Analyzes order volume by age group and gender:
  - Adult women form the largest customer base (34.59%).
  - Teenage girls and adult men are secondary audiences.


### 7. **Sales: Top 5 States (Horizontal Bar Chart)**
- Top contributing states by sales amount:
  - **Maharashtra**: ₹2.99M (Highest)
  - **Karnataka**: ₹2.65M
  - **Uttar Pradesh**: ₹2.10M
  - **Telangana**: ₹1.71M
  - **Tamil Nadu**: ₹1.68M
- Useful for regional performance targeting and expansion planning.


## 📌 Key Insights
- Women's products dominate both in order volume and sales.
- Amazon and Flipkart are the leading sales channels.
- Sales peak in Q1, especially March, and decline towards the end of the year.
- Maharashtra leads in regional sales, suggesting a strong customer base there.
- Returns and refunds are minimal compared to total deliveries.
